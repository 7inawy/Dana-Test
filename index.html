The Story Behind Dana

A Tuesday night.
A worried mother.

"

Mariam's 4-month-old has been crying for hours. She searches in Arabic — nothing useful. She calls three clinics — none available until Thursday. She can't remember which vaccines he's had. She has no idea if this is normal development for his age.

She is not alone. She is every Arabic-speaking parent, every night.

— A scenario played out in millions of homes across Egypt every week.

Dana was built for Mariam.

Architecture Decision: AuthInterceptor
All API calls are routed through a single AuthInterceptor that automatically injects JWT tokens and handles 401 responses — redirecting to login and clearing secure storage. Public paths (signup, signin, Google OAuth) are whitelisted and bypass the Authorization header entirely.
